It is hard to believe a full year has passed since our wedding day in September 2025. To celebrate surviving and thriving through our first year of marriage, the two of us packed our bags for a weekend escape down to Cape May. We wanted a trip where we could just slow down, step away from the daily grind, and truly enjoy each other's company, and this historic seaside town delivered exactly that.

We anchored our stay at the Sea Crest Inn, which put us right where we needed to be and set a relaxing tone for the entire weekend. We spent plenty of time down by the water, soaking in the late summer ocean breeze at Sunset Beach. There is something incredibly grounding about walking the shoreline together, reflecting on everything from our wedding day to all the wild home projects and adventures we have shared since. The weather was perfect for simply unwinding on the sand and taking a breather.

When we were not by the ocean, we found ourselves wandering through the heart of town. We spent hours doing lots of shopping along the Washington Street Mall, popping in and out of the local boutiques and picking up a few keepsakes. The pedestrian square has such a vibrant and welcoming energy, making it easy to just stroll hand in hand and get lost in the afternoon. We also made sure to treat ourselves to some incredible food, including a memorable stop at the Washington Inn.

Of course, no celebration is complete without raising a glass. We spent some time at Naughty Spirits, taking in the great atmosphere and enjoying some fantastic drinks. We even made sure to grab a round at the Ugly Mug to keep the fun going. It was the perfect way to kick back, share some laughs, and toast to our first year as husband and wife.
